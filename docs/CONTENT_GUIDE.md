# Church Secretary Content Guide — Tabernacle Community Baptist Church

Welcome to your new church website admin portal! This guide explains how to update service times, publish weekly announcements, upload sermons, and manage event details without writing code.

---

## Accessing the Admin Portal
1. Go to `https://www.tcb-church.com/admin`
2. Sign in with your registered church email address.

---

## Publishing Weekly Announcements
1. Click **Announcements** on your admin dashboard.
2. Click **Post Announcement**.
3. Type the announcement title and text.
4. Check **Pin to home page** if this announcement should appear at the top of the home page.
5. Click **Publish Announcement**.

---

## Adding Events & Service Times
1. Click **Events & Services**.
2. Fill in the event title, date, start time, and location (e.g. Main Sanctuary or Call-in Line).
3. Click **Publish Event**.

---

## Updating Sermons & Livestream
1. Click **Sermons & Media**.
2. Paste the Facebook Live video URL or YouTube video link.
3. Click **Publish Sermon**.

---

## Image Upload Guidelines
- Photos automatically convert to fast WebP images.
- Keep photo file sizes under 5 MB.
- Neutral, warm church sanctuary images work best.
